## Phase 2 Plan — Custom Data Model (Detailed)

### Backend Modules to Create (in order)
Each follows Twenty's pattern: entity → module → service → resolver → controller → migration

1. **policy** — Insurance policies
   - Fields: policyNumber, type (enum: commercial/industrial/liability/etc), startDate, endDate, premium, status (active/expired/cancelled/pending), coverageDetails, insurer, notes
   - Relations: company (many-to-one), person (many-to-one)

2. **claim** — Insurance claims
   - Fields: claimNumber, date, amount, status (open/closed/pending), description, resolution
   - Relations: policy (many-to-one), company, person

3. **renewal** — Policy renewals
   - Fields: renewalDate, newPremium, status (pending/accepted/declined), notes
   - Relations: policy (many-to-one)

4. **risk-profile** — Client risk assessment
   - Fields: riskCategory (enum), assessmentDate, score, notes
   - Relations: company (many-to-one), person (many-to-one)

5. **premium** — Premium tracking
   - Fields: amount, period (monthly/quarterly/annual), paymentStatus (paid/unpaid/overdue), dueDate, paidDate
   - Relations: policy (many-to-one)

6. **provision** — Broker commission/bonus-malus
   - Fields: commissionRate, bonusMalusTier, amount, status, period
   - Relations: policy (many-to-one), workspace-member (broker)
